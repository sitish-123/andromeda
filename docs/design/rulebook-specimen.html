2 · Foundations

Scales

Space

2 only inside controls of 32 or less. Everything else is on the 4 grid (R1).

Radius

Capsule = height / 2. Nested radius = outer radius - padding (R7).

Heights

Controls 24 / 32 / 40 / 48. Rows 32 / 40 (one line), 48 / 56 (two lines). Padding follows: (height - line) / 2 (R2).

Concentric (R7)

Sheet r 28, p 12 → tile r 16, p 8 → image r 8. The dock: r 24, inset 4 → lens r 20. The island: 32 capsule, inset 4 → 24 capsule.

7 · Motion

Twelve springs

k = (2π / d)², z = 1 - b, mass 1. Each plot shows 0.8 s. Spatial springs (blue) may bounce; effect springs (grey) never do.

Grow from the island, morph in place

Click the right island (or drag down from it): the sheet grows out of it, keeps its right edge, and returns into it. Click the center island buttons to morph it. Click again during any motion: it turns around from its current velocity.

7.6 P10 · P11 · P12 · P15 · P16

Menus, dialogs, pages, windows

P10 Menu from its button · P12 Pages

The menu grows from the button corner (or the pointer: right-click the area). The current item is the lens. Drag the pages: 1:1, rubber band at the ends, release projects and settles on page with your velocity.

P15 Window from its dock icon · P16 minimize, close · P11 dialog

Click a dock icon: the window grows out of it. Minimize returns into the icon; close shrinks to 0.96 and fades. "Delete" opens an alert from the button.
